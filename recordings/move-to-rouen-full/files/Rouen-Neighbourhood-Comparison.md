**For:** Non-EU/EEA NEOMA student, arriving Rouen 4 January 2027
**Campus:** NEOMA Rouen (Mont-Saint-Aignan plateau)
**Research date:** 23 October 2026 — web research, no single official per-neighbourhood rent/crime dataset exists for these areas (see notes). Re-check prices close to your arrival.

## Recommendation

**Mont-Saint-Aignan is the best fit for your budget and goals.** It gives you the shortest commute (you could even walk), a real sub-€600 private-market option or the NEOMA/CROUS-partnered Panorama residence at €268/month as a safety net, and a strong built-in student community. Rouen Centre is the best **second choice** if you want more city life and are willing to add 10–20 minutes of commute — rents still fit your budget. Bois-Guillaume is the safest and quietest, but its studio supply is too thin to rely on as a first choice — only pursue it opportunistically.

## Checklist: shortlisting a studio
- [ ] Confirm budget includes charges (utilities) — ask explicitly, as "charges comprises" varies by listing
- [ ] Check distance to nearest NEOMA bus stop (F7, F2, T5) if not in Mont-Saint-Aignan
- [ ] Ask for an unfurnished vs furnished breakdown — furnished studios often suit short student stays better
- [ ] Verify you can provide a French guarantor's documents (you already have a guarantor — confirm their income/tax documents are ready)
- [ ] Check if the building/landlord accepts CAF housing aid tenants
- [ ] Ask about notice period and lease type (bail étudiant / bail mobilité vs classic 3-year bail)

## Deadlines to track (see also the admin checklist poster and calendar)
- Sign lease / get keys: as close to 4 January 2027 as possible
- Home insurance (assurance habitation) must be in place **before** you get the keys — it's a legal requirement for tenants in France <cite url="https://www.service-public.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F31300"/>
- CAF housing aid (APL) application: submit as soon as you sign the lease — there's no retroactivity, so every month you delay is lost aid <cite url="https://www.caf.fr/allocataires/caf-de-paris/actualites-departementales/tout-savoir-sur-l-aide-au-logement-etudiant"/>

## What to verify yourself
- Exact studio rents change week to week — re-check LocService, SeLoger, PAP and leboncoin close to your move-in date.
- No official crime dataset was found for Mont-Saint-Aignan or Rouen Centre — treat safety comments as anecdotal.
- Guarantor requirements can vary by landlord — confirm with each landlord what documents they want from your guarantor.
