## Our take
- **If your priority is the shortest commute and lowest cost**: Mont-Saint-Aignan wins — it's where the campus actually sits, rents are the cheapest of the three, and the student housing market is large.
- **If your priority is city life and walkability**: Vieux-Marché/Cathédrale wins — but budget for a smaller unit and accept a 15–25 min bus commute.
- **If your priority is calm and residential feel**: Bois-Guillaume is a solid middle ground — no river crossing to campus, quiet streets — but expect fewer studios on the market at any given time, so start looking early and move fast.

**What we couldn't confirm** (verify yourself before committing): exact walking times from a specific address to campus, official crime statistics for any of the three areas, and typical monthly charges (water/electricity/heating) on top of listed rent. Use a routing app (Google Maps/Citymapper) for your exact candidate address, and ask each landlord directly about charges.
