# Admin Deadlines — NEOMA Rouen, Starting 4 January

All timings below are counted from your actual arrival date in France (not 4 January specifically, if you land earlier or later). Confirm every rule with the official body — French admin rules can change and some depend on your nationality/visa type.

## 1. OFII — validating your long-stay visa (VLS-TS)

**What it is:** OFII (Office Français de l'Immigration et de l'Intégration) is the office that validates your long-stay student visa so it legally acts as your residence permit for year 1.

- **Deadline: within 3 months of your first entry into France.** <cite url="https://www.campusfrance.org/fr/comment-valider-votre-visa-long-sejour-a-votre-arrivee-en-france"/>
- Done **entirely online** via the ANEF portal (demarches-simplifiees / demarches.interieur.gouv.fr). <cite url="https://demarchesetrangers.fr/etudiant/vls-ts-validation-etudiant"/>
- You will need to pay a tax (reported around €100 + a €50 stamp, i.e. ~€150 total — confirm the exact current amount on the official site, as fees can change). <cite url="https://demarchesetrangers.fr/etudiant/vls-ts-validation-etudiant"/>
- After this deadline, validation reportedly becomes impossible, so don't delay. <cite url="https://www.integrationfrance.fr/articles/visa-long-sejour-vls-ts-2026-validation-ofii-taxe-droits"/>
- Official source: <cite url="https://www.ofii.fr/validation-vlsts-et-convocation-ofii/"/>, <cite url="https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/R52684?lang=en"/>

**Your action:** If you arrive 4 January, your deadline is roughly **early April** — but start the online form in your first week, since OFII may also convoke you for a medical visit/appointment.

## 2. CAF — housing aid (APL)

**What it is:** CAF (Caisse d'Allocations Familiales) can pay you monthly housing aid (APL) that reduces your effective rent, if your studio is eligible (CROUS and most private rentals generally are).

- Your right to aid starts the **month after you move in and apply** — there is no retroactive payment since 2011. <cite url="https://www.aide-sociale.fr/apl-retroactif/"/>
- Apply **as soon as you sign your lease and move in** — don't wait.
- Expect **roughly 2–4 weeks processing**, sometimes **4–10 weeks** for the first payment to actually land, especially as an international student submitting documents for the first time. <cite url="https://demarchesadministratives.fr/actualites/demande-apl-combien-de-temps-faut-il-attendre-avant-le-premier-versement"/> <cite url="https://dalili.study/blog/caf-etudiant-etranger-delais-documents-erreurs"/>
- Example: moving in and applying in January means your first payment likely won't arrive until February at the earliest. <cite url="https://www.nemea-residence-etudiante.com/fr/preparer-mon-emmenagement/quand-faire-sa-demande-d-apl-pour-son-logement-etudiant"/>

**Your action:** Apply on caf.fr the same week you sign your lease (around 4 January). You'll need your lease, bank details (RIB), and your residence permit/visa info.

## 3. Bank account

**What it is:** You'll need a French bank account (RIB) for CAF, rent payments, and daily life.

- Required documents typically: valid passport, proof of address in France, proof of enrolment/student card, and your visa/VLS-TS (validated or in process). <cite url="https://www.campusfrance.org/fr/ouvrir-un-compte-bancaire-en-france"/>
- Some banks accept your OFII validation attestation or visa as ID for students. <cite url="https://dalili.study/blog/ouvrir-compte-bancaire-etudiant-etranger-2026"/>
- No fixed legal deadline, but **do it in your first 1–2 weeks** — CAF, insurance and your landlord's rent transfer all depend on it.

**Your action:** Open an account within your first two weeks. You'll need a "justificatif de domicile" (proof of address) — your signed lease or an attestation from your residence usually works.

## 4. Health insurance (Sécurité Sociale étudiante)

**What it is:** Affiliation to the French public health insurance (Assurance Maladie / "Sécu") is free and compulsory for all students, including international students, so you get reimbursed for doctor visits and prescriptions. <cite url="https://etudiant-etranger.ameli.fr/assistance"/>

- Non-EU students register on the dedicated portal **etudiant-etranger.ameli.fr**, available in French, English and Spanish. <cite url="https://etudiant-etranger.ameli.fr/"/>
- You can register **as soon as you are enrolled at NEOMA** — do this in your first week. <cite url="https://www.ameli.fr/assure/droits-demarches/europe-international/protection-sociale-france/vous-venez-etudier-en-france"/>
- It is free and separate from any private/complementary "mutuelle" top-up insurance, which is optional but recommended. <cite url="https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/R54512"/>
- EU/EEA students: bring your European Health Insurance Card (EHIC) instead — confirm with your home country's health authority.

**Your action:** Register on etudiant-etranger.ameli.fr in week 1; keep checking for your social security number, which some banks/CAF steps may ask for.

## 5. Renter's insurance (assurance habitation) — required before you get your keys

**What it is:** French landlords are legally entitled to ask for proof of home insurance ("attestation d'assurance habitation") before handing over the keys, and most require it on the day you sign the lease ("état des lieux d'entrée"). This is separate from health insurance.

- Arrange this **before or on move-in day**, not after — many landlords will not let you collect the keys without it.
- You can usually get a basic student policy for a few euros per month from major French insurers (banks also often offer one when you open your account).
- **This is standard practice, but confirm the exact requirement with your specific landlord** — it is normally written into your lease ("bail").

## 6. CVEC — student life and campus contribution

**What it is:** CVEC (Contribution Vie Étudiante et de Campus) is a mandatory fee that most students enrolling in French higher education must pay before finalizing their registration, funding student health/culture/sports services.

- Pay it online and get your attestation **before or shortly after enrolling at NEOMA** — the school will likely ask for proof of payment as part of your enrolment file.
- Check directly with NEOMA's international student office whether and when this applies to your specific program, since some private schools/exchange situations have different rules.

## Your timeline at a glance

| When | Action |
|---|---|
| Before 4 Jan | Sign lease, prepare documents (passport, visa, NEOMA enrolment certificate, Visale/guarantor) |
| Week 1 (arrival, ~4 Jan) | Open bank account; register for Sécu on etudiant-etranger.ameli.fr; start OFII online validation |
| Week 1–2 | Apply for CAF housing aid (APL) with your signed lease and RIB |
| Within 3 months of arrival | OFII validation must be completed (hard legal deadline) |
| Ongoing | Watch for OFII medical appointment convocation; track CAF payment status online |

## Important note
These are general rules for international students; your exact timeline can depend on your nationality, visa type, and NEOMA's own enrolment calendar. **Confirm your personal deadlines with OFII, CAF, your bank, and ameli.fr directly, and check with NEOMA's international student office for any school-specific guidance.**
