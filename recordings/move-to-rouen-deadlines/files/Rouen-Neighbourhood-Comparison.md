## Recommendation: **Mont-Saint-Aignan**

For your priorities (budget <€650, starting mid-year in January, wanting a simple daily routine), Mont-Saint-Aignan wins mainly on **commute**: you can walk or cycle to class in 5–15 minutes instead of budgeting 20–40 minutes each way on a bus line, which matters a lot on a cold January morning. <cite url="https://neoma-bs.com/about-neoma/campuses/rouen"/> Rents there (€435–560/month on the open market, or €595–645 for a furnished all-inclusive private student residence like Neoresid or Twenty Campus) comfortably fit your budget. <cite url="https://www.immojeune.com/logement-etudiant/mont-saint-aignan-76.html"/>

**One caution:** avoid renting directly on or overlooking Place Colbert itself — local press has reported recurring youth-gang disturbances there since 2024. Streets a block or two away (closer to the university/NEOMA cluster or Côte Sainte-Catherine) are reported as calmer. <cite url="https://www.paris-normandie.fr/id569558/article/2024-10-17/au-nord-de-rouen-quelles-reponses-linsecurite-qui-inquiete-les-habitants-de"/>

**Good runner-up:** If you want more city life (restaurants, bars, shops) and don't mind a 20–30 minute commute, **Pasteur** in Rouen's centre is lively, well-connected, and still affordable (€450–600). <cite url="https://rouenonest.fr/quartier-pasteur/"/>

**Private residences worth contacting directly, since they can onboard mid-year (unlike most CROUS leases which run Sept–June):**
- Neoresid Mont-Saint-Aignan — studio ~€595 CC <cite url="https://www.immojeune.com/logement-etudiant/mont-saint-aignan-76.html"/>
- Twenty Campus Mont-Saint-Aignan — studio ~€645 CC, all-inclusive (breakfast, cleaning, gym, laundry) <cite url="https://www.immojeune.com/logement-etudiant/mont-saint-aignan-76.html"/>
- Nemea Appart'Etud Rouen Constantine — studio ~€528 CC, near campus <cite url="https://www.studylease.com/residences-etudiantes-universite-de-rouen"/>

**CROUS note:** CROUS (public, cheapest at €370–450/month) allocates most rooms via the Dossier Social Étudiant between March and May for a September start, so a January arrival is outside the normal cycle — contact CROUS Normandie directly (resid-panorama@crous-normandie.fr, 02 32 10 09 04) to ask about mid-year vacancies or short-stay housing. <cite url="https://www.lescrous.fr/nos-services/notre-offre-commerciale/hebergement-courts-sejours-des-logements-a-tarif-crous-pour-une-courte-duree/"/>

## Checklist: finding your studio (before 4 January)

- [ ] Shortlist listings on SeLoger, LeBonCoin, PAP, Bien'ici, ImmoJeune, LocService — filter "Mont-Saint-Aignan 76130" or "Pasteur/Rouen 76000"
- [ ] Contact Neoresid and Twenty Campus directly about January availability (private residences, no CROUS-style deadline)
- [ ] Prepare your rental file: passport, proof of NEOMA enrolment, proof of income/scholarship, French guarantor OR Visale guarantee (free state guarantee for students — check visale.fr) or Garantme
- [ ] Ask every landlord: is the "charges comprises" figure final, or are there extra utility bills?
- [ ] Avoid signing for anything directly overlooking Place Colbert without visiting in the evening first
- [ ] Budget a security deposit (usually 1 month's rent) + possible agency fees
